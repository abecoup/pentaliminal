Product roadmap.

Five phases, one product at a time. Each phase has to pass a test before the next one starts; the diamonds below are those tests. We start with Trust because it answers a question companies already face: can we prove what our AI did?

Plans change as we learn, and this page changes with them. Features and technical detail will fill in product by product.

01Phase 1

Pentaliminal Trust

Proof, provenance and protection for every AI action in a company.

{{STATUS:trust}}{{TRACK:trust}}
Who it’s for
Companies running AI agents on customer or financial records.
What it does
  • Ledger. Every prompt and agent step, linked to the person who started it.
  • Guard. AI you didn’t invite is kept out, and your own agents are kept within limits.
  • Reports. Audit packets, plus usage by team, person and agent.
Where it fits
Leads the Agency part of our infrastructure, and draws most on Acting and Knowing.

02Phase 2

Pentaliminal Notebook

An AI-native notebook built for learning, not just storing.

{{STATUS:notebook}}{{TRACK:notebook}}
Who it’s for
Students, researchers and writers: anyone who works with ideas.
Where it fits
Leads the Cognitive part of our infrastructure, and draws most on Knowing and Situating.

03Phase 3

Pentaliminal Rhetor

A practice room for speaking, communicating and telling stories.

{{STATUS:rhetor}}{{TRACK:rhetor}}
Who it’s for
Anyone who has to speak well: students, teachers, professionals, and Forum’s future hosts.
What it does
  • Study. A great speaker broken down into a blueprint of their words, sentence shapes, moves, structure and delivery.
  • Practice. Imitate a passage side by side with your camera, one skill at a time, looping the hard parts like a musician.
  • Feedback. Scored against the model on rhythm and delivery, and on whether your message and story landed.
Holds a line
It teaches persuasion that rests on evidence, and names the tricks that work without it.
Where it fits
Serves one of our principles: preserve human agency. Draws most on Seeing and Valuing.

04Phase 4

Pentaliminal Forum

A home for live debates, talks and podcasts about hard questions.

{{STATUS:forum}}{{TRACK:forum}}
Who it’s for
Academics, teachers, students and curious listeners, plus conferences that want to stream.
What it does
  • Live. Debates, talks, chat rooms and streamed events.
  • Kept. Every session recorded, transcribed and split into chapters.
  • Mapped. Conversations tied to questions, so you can follow a disagreement across many of them.
Where it fits
Leads the Orientation part of our infrastructure, and draws most on Seeing and Valuing.

05Phase 5

Open the shared memory.

Every product reads and writes the same four objects. Once all four products have tested them, we publish them as an open standard and API, so other AI systems can build on them too.

{{STATUS:memory}}{{TRACK:memory}}
Why it matters
With these four objects in one place, our principles become working parts. “Make uncertainty visible” becomes a status on every claim, and “make trust operational” becomes a review record.
Why it comes last
A standard is only worth publishing once four products have tested it.
How
Model-agnostic wherever that makes sense.
Where it fits
This is the infrastructure itself: the part other systems can use, not just our own products.
