# Pentaliminal brand foundations

Positioning and charter from Abraham's research, November 2026. These are used on the V2 site (sections 01, 02 and 06).

## The intersection

Pentaliminal occupies the intersection of three things:

1. **The science of intelligence**: understanding how intelligence works.
2. **The wisdom of humanity**: understanding what makes judgment, action, and civilization worthwhile.
3. **The engineering of AI**: turning those understandings into useful technological infrastructure.

Together: **Pentaliminal, infrastructure for wise intelligence.**

Visual: a tetrahedron. The three base corners are the three fields; the apex where their edges meet is Pentaliminal.

## Charter

- **The belief**: Intelligence should be developed with an understanding of what it means to be human, what makes life meaningful, and what is worth pursuing.
- **The mission**: To advance the science and engineering of wise intelligence through interdisciplinary research and technological innovation.
- **The vision**: A world in which human and artificial intelligence work together to address humanity's most important challenges and expand the possibilities of human flourishing.
- **The method**: Integrating cognitive science, psychology, anthropology, philosophy, and systems thinking into the design and development of AI.
- **The ambition**: To build the cognitive and decision infrastructure of the next era of intelligence.

## From solutionism to problem wisdom

| Conventional approach | Pentaliminal approach |
|---|---|
| What can we build with AI? | What is worth building, and why? |

## The Human Problem Atlas

A proposed Pentaliminal research initiative: an evolving knowledge platform that investigates the most consequential problems facing humanity.

| Domain | Fundamental question |
|---|---|
| Human development | How do we help people realize their potential? |
| Mental and social well-being | How do we reduce suffering and improve relationships? |
| Education | How do we develop more capable, curious, thoughtful people? |
| Institutional intelligence | How do societies make better collective decisions? |
| Scientific discovery | How can we accelerate the discovery of important truths? |
| Environmental systems | How do we maintain the conditions for life to flourish? |
| Economic opportunity | How do we distribute opportunity and enable meaningful contribution? |
| Information ecosystems | How do we improve access to trustworthy knowledge and reduce manipulation? |
